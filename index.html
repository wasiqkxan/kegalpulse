Everything you need to build the habit

One short session a day, structured so you train the full range — not just one move.

A guided 30-day program

Start where it makes sense for you and build gradually — steady holds, quick contractions, staged “elevator” holds, reverse-kegel releases, and diaphragmatic breathing.

Guided session player

A calm breathing circle paces every rep through SQUEEZE, RELAX and REST, with a live rep and set counter. The screen stays awake so you're never interrupted mid-session.

Visual, sound or vibration cues

Pick whichever fits the moment. Voice-guided prompts keep your eyes off the screen when you'd rather not look.

Discreet mode

A near-black, low-key screen lets you practise anywhere — a desk, a waiting room, a commute — without drawing attention.

Progress you can see

A calendar heat-map, weekly chart, best streak, total sessions and minutes, plus a Pelvic Fitness Score and achievement badges.

Reminders that you control

Up to three daily reminders, set to your own times. Streak tracking keeps the momentum going — and none of it costs anything.

Bonus sessions — all free

Relax & Breathe, Office Break, Sleep Routine, Fast Twitch and Deep Session. No unlocks, no paywalls, nothing held back.

Learn the basics

Short, plain-language articles help you find the right muscles and understand good technique before you build the habit.

A look inside

Calm, clinical and uncluttered — built to get out of your way.

Private by design

Your pelvic health data never leaves your phone

There is no account and no server. Your sessions, streaks, progress and settings are stored only on your device — nothing about them is ever uploaded, shared, or used to target ads.

Read the full privacy policy
  • No login, no email, no profile. The app never asks who you are.
  • No cloud sync. There is no backend that could receive your practice data.
  • Ad-supported, kept separate. Ads are served by Google AdMob and are never shown during a session or during setup. No pelvic health information is used to target them.
  • Anonymous diagnostics only. Firebase collects usage counts and crash reports so the app keeps working — never the content of your sessions or assessment answers.
  • Uninstall clears everything. Because it's all local, removing the app removes the data.
