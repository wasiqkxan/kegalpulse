Privacy Policy

Kegel Pulse — Pelvic Trainer · Effective 1 August 2026

Your pelvic health data never leaves your device. Kegel Pulse has no account system, no login, and no backend server. The app does show ads and collect anonymous usage statistics, both of which are described in full below.

What stays on your device

Everything you create in Kegel Pulse — your workout sessions, streaks, progress statistics, assessment answers, reminders, and settings — is stored only on your device. None of it is ever uploaded. We have no server that could receive it, and no way to see it.

Advertising

Kegel Pulse displays ads supplied by Google AdMob. To select and measure ads, Google may access your device's advertising ID along with technical information such as your device model, general (country-level) location, and IP address.

Ads are never shown during an exercise session or while you are setting the app up for the first time. No information about your pelvic health, your progress, or your assessment answers is used to target ads or shared with advertisers.

You can reset or delete your advertising ID at any time in your device's Settings → Privacy → Ads. If you are in the EEA or UK, the app asks for your consent before requesting personalised ads, and you can change or withdraw that consent at any time via Settings → Privacy → Ad privacy options inside the app.

Google's handling of this data is governed by the Google Privacy & Terms.

Anonymous usage statistics and crash reports

We use Google Firebase (Analytics, Crashlytics and Performance Monitoring) to understand how the app is used and to find bugs. This collects:

  • Which screens are opened, and counts of events such as starting or completing a session
  • Session duration and streak length as numbers only
  • Crash reports, error diagnostics and app performance timings
  • An app instance identifier, device model, operating system version and country

This data is not linked to your name, email or any account, because the app has none. It never includes the content of your assessment answers or any pelvic health information.

If you are in the EEA or UK, the consent choices you make when you first open the app — reachable again at any time via Settings → Privacy → Ad privacy options — govern this collection as well as advertising.

Permissions the app uses

  • Notifications — used solely to send the daily practice reminders you schedule inside the app. You can turn these off at any time.
  • Vibration — used for the optional haptic cues during a session.
  • Internet — used to load ads and send anonymous usage statistics. Your practice data is never sent.

Children

Kegel Pulse is not directed at children and is not intended for use by anyone under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

Your choices

  • Change or withdraw consent (EEA/UK): Settings → Privacy → Ad privacy options
  • Reset your advertising ID: your device's system settings
  • Delete all your practice data: Settings → Reset progress, or uninstall the app

Uninstalling

Because all of your practice data is stored locally, uninstalling the app permanently removes it.
